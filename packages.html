The Himalayan Pilgrimage

What Is the Char Dham Yatra?

The Char Dham Yatra is a pilgrimage to Uttarakhand's four most sacred Himalayan shrines — Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. Together they mark the origins of the Yamuna and Ganga rivers and two of Hinduism's most revered temples, Kedarnath and Badrinath. Kafall Travels plans the entire circuit door-to-door from Delhi, so you can focus on the yatra while we handle permits, stays, cabs and the route.

How It Flows

Full Char Dham Yatra, Day by Day

01

Delhi to Yamunotri

Drive to Barkot via Haridwar, then trek or pony ride to Yamunotri for darshan at the Yamuna's source and the Surya Kund hot springs.

02

Yamunotri to Gangotri

Cross over to Uttarkashi and on to Gangotri, where the Bhagirathi emerges beside the temple, with the Gaumukh glacier trek optional.

03

Gangotri to Kedarnath

Travel to Sonprayag and Gaurikund, then trek roughly 11 km to Kedarnath for darshan at the Jyotirlinga above the treeline.

04

Kedarnath to Badrinath

Descend and drive via Rudraprayag to Badrinath, the final shrine, with Mana village and the Neelkanth peak nearby before the return to Delhi.

Char Dham Yatra Options, Explained

The complete Full Char Dham Yatra package covers all four shrines — Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath — in the traditional west-to-east order over roughly 10 to 11 days from Delhi. Travellers short on time or leave often choose the Do Dham Yatra package, covering just Kedarnath and Badrinath, the two most-visited shrines of the circuit.

If you'd rather focus on a single shrine, standalone Yamunotri and Gangotri packages are available, alongside our dedicated Kedarnath Yatra package and Badrinath Yatra package. For senior citizens or anyone wanting to complete the yatra in days rather than weeks, the Helicopter Char Dham Yatra covers all four shrines by air with road transfers at each stop.
