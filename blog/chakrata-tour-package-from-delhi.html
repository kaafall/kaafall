    Places to Visit in Chakrata

    A longer Chakrata 4 days 3 nights package lets you explore beyond the standard weekend circuit. Here are the top places to include:

    Tiger Falls

    Ideal for nature lovers and the most popular stop. Comfortable footwear recommended.

    Kanasar Forest

    Known for impressive deodar trees and a quiet outdoor experience.

    Chilmiri Neck

    A relaxed evening spot for photography and mountain views.

    Budher Caves & Moila Top

    For adventure lovers. The Budher Caves trek and Moila Top route are more demanding, so check trail and weather conditions first.

    Other places worth considering:

    • Lakhamandal Temple for history and religious sites
    • Mundali Chakrata for mountain scenery and seasonal snow
    • Deoban for forests, viewpoints and nature

    You don't need to fit everything into one short trip. For a 3-day package, Tiger Falls, Deoban and Chilmiri Neck are the most practical; a fourth day gives flexibility for Budher Caves, Moila Top or nearby excursions.

    Best Time to Visit Chakrata and Weather

    The best time to visit Chakrata depends on what you want from the trip. March to June suits comfortable sightseeing, while September to November brings pleasant weather and clearer mountain views.

    SeasonChakrata WeatherBest For
    March–JunePleasant to moderately warmSightseeing, family trips, trekking
    July–AugustMonsoon, frequent rain possibleGreen landscapes
    September–NovemberCool and pleasantViews, outdoor activities
    December–FebruaryCold, possible snowfallSnow trips and winter travel

    For Chakrata snowfall, most travellers plan between late December and February. Snowfall is a natural event and cannot be guaranteed for specific dates, and it can affect access to higher spots like Deoban and Mundali. Check live weather on IMD before you leave.

    How to Reach Chakrata from Delhi

    For how to reach Chakrata, road travel is the most convenient option from Delhi. A common route is:

    Delhi → Meerut → Muzaffarnagar → Dehradun → Vikasnagar → Kalsi → Chakrata

    About 320–340 km · 8–10 hours

    A private cab works well for couples and families, while a tempo traveller is economical for a Chakrata group tour package. On public transport, first reach Dehradun, then continue to Chakrata by local transport or taxi.

    Mountain roads are slower than plains, so don't plan around distance alone. Keep buffer time, especially on weekends, in winter and during monsoon. Also read our guide on how to reach Munsiyari from Delhi if you plan a longer Kumaon trip next.

    Things to Do in Chakrata & Booking Tips for 2027

    The best things to do in Chakrata include waterfall visits, short treks, forest drives, photography, camping, local sightseeing and simply enjoying quiet mountain surroundings.

    Travellers often compare Chakrata vs Mussoorie. Mussoorie offers more hotels, cafes, shopping and tourist infrastructure, whereas Chakrata suits those who want forests, trekking and a less commercial experience.

    Before confirming your 2027 booking, compare packages on actual inclusions, not price alone:

    • Hotel location and room category
    • Vehicle type and seating capacity
    • Sightseeing coverage and meal plan
    • Pickup point and cancellation terms
    • For groups: how many people share each room
