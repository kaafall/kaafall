Planning a Chopta Tungnath tour package? Chopta is one of Uttarakhand's most beautiful mountain destinations, surrounded by forests, alpine meadows and Himalayan views. Located in the Rudraprayag region, it is popular for Tungnath Temple, Chandrashila Summit and Deoria Tal.

Whether you want a peaceful mountain holiday, a Chopta weekend trip, camping or your first Himalayan trek, this Chopta travel guide covers the best places to visit, weather, snowfall, hotels, trekking routes, ideal season and a practical itinerary.

In this guide

    Chopta Travel Guide: Quick Information

    Chopta is often called the Mini Switzerland of Uttarakhand for its green meadows and mountain scenery. It is also the base for the Chopta Tungnath Chandrashila Trek, one of the best-known short Himalayan treks in the state. A Chopta tour package is the easiest way to combine stay, transport and trek in one plan.

    Chopta is not a large commercial hill station. Its appeal lies in forests, mountain trails and simple accommodation, which makes it quite different from Mussoorie or Nainital.

    Best Places to Visit in Chopta

    The best places to visit in Chopta are spread across the mountain region, not around a single market. Tungnath and Chandrashila are the highlights, but Deoria Tal, Sari Village and the nearby forests deserve time too. Our Chopta Tungnath tour packages cover these key spots.

    1

    Tungnath Temple

    The highlight of most trips and part of the Panch Kedar tradition. It is reached by a mountain trek that begins near Chopta.

    2

    Chandrashila Summit

    Continue from Tungnath to Chandrashila, known for panoramic Himalayan views when the weather is clear.

    3

    Deoria Tal

    Deoria Tal, usually approached via Sari Village, is a forest-fringed lake with views of the Chaukhamba range on clear days.

    4

    Sari Village

    The starting point for the Deoria Tal trek and a good place to feel village life in the Garhwal Himalayas.

    The wider region is part of the Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary, so its forests and mountain ecosystem are a big part of the Chopta experience.

    Chopta Tungnath Chandrashila Trek

    For many travellers this trek is the main reason to visit. Chopta to Tungnath is short compared with multi-day Himalayan treks, but the continuous uphill climb and altitude can still be demanding. From Tungnath the trail continues to Chandrashila. Weather changes quickly at height, so carry proper shoes, warm clothing and enough water.

    Trek SectionApprox. DistanceDifficulty
    Chopta to TungnathAround 3.5 kmEasy to Moderate
    Tungnath to ChandrashilaAround 1–1.5 kmModerate
    Sari Village to Deoria TalAround 2–3 kmEasy to Moderate
    Complete Tungnath–Chandrashila routeAround 9–10 km returnModerate

    Beginners should not treat this like a normal sightseeing walk. Snow, rain, ice and altitude can change the difficulty a lot. In winter the same route becomes a popular Chopta snow trek, but snow-covered trails need more preparation and take much longer.     Chopta Snowfall and Weather

    Chopta snowfall is one of the most searched reasons to visit in winter. Snow is generally possible from December to February, but exact dates and snow depth depend on current weather. December and January attract travellers looking for a Chopta winter trek, snowy landscapes and winter camping. Heavy snowfall can temporarily affect roads and trekking routes.

    SeasonWeatherTravel Experience
    March–AprilCold to pleasantTrekking and spring scenery
    May–JunePleasantFamily trips and sightseeing
    July–AugustRainyGreen landscapes but difficult roads/trails
    September–NovemberCool and generally clearerTrekking and mountain views
    December–FebruaryVery cold, snow possibleSnow trips and winter trekking

    Spring is attractive when rhododendron forests add colour, while autumn is preferred for clearer Himalayan views. Check live conditions on IMD before you travel.

    Best Time to Visit Chopta

    There is no single best time; it depends on what you want. For comfortable sightseeing and trekking, April to June is popular. September to November is another excellent window for outdoor activities and clear views. For snow, most travellers prefer December to February.

    Monsoon brings greenery but also heavy rain, slippery trails, landslides and road disruption, so check local conditions first. Access to Tungnath Temple is seasonal, so do not plan a winter visit only around darshan; winter trips are mainly for snow and trekking. Read the official Uttarakhand Tourism site for current advisories.

    Hotels, Camping and Where to Stay in Chopta

    Stay options are small hotels, guesthouses, homestays and camps rather than big luxury resorts. Baniyakund and Dugalbitta are popular for accommodation, and Sari Village suits the Deoria Tal trek. A Chopta camping stay can be great in good weather, but confirm bedding, toilets, electricity and hot water before booking.

    • Rooms fill quickly on weekends, summer holidays and in snow season – book early
    • Internet and electricity can be limited compared with major hill stations

    Frequently Asked Questions About Chopta

    What is the best time to visit Chopta?

    April to June is best for pleasant weather and September to November for trekking and clearer Himalayan views. For snow, December to February is generally preferred.

    When does snowfall happen in Chopta?

    Chopta snowfall is usually possible between December and February, with January often a popular month. Timing and intensity vary every year.

    How many days are enough for a Chopta trip?

    A 3 to 4-day trip is ideal for Chopta, Tungnath, Chandrashila, Deoria Tal and nearby attractions without rushing.

    How difficult is the Chopta Tungnath Chandrashila trek?

    It is generally easy to moderate, but the steep climb, altitude, snow and changing weather can make it more challenging.

    Where should I stay in Chopta?

    You can find hotels, homestays, guesthouses and camps around Chopta, Baniyakund and Dugalbitta. Sari Village is convenient for Deoria Tal.

    What are the best places to visit in Chopta?

    Tungnath Temple, Chandrashila Summit, Deoria Tal, Sari Village, Ukhimath, Baniyakund and Dugalbitta.

    Chopta is best enjoyed without overcrowding the itinerary. Keep enough time for Tungnath, Chandrashila and Deoria Tal, and check weather and road conditions before departure. Ready to go?
