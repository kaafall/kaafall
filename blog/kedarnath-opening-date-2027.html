    When Will Kedarnath Temple Open in 2027?

    The Kedarnath Temple opening date 2027 is expected in the first half of May, with the most likely planning window around 9–12 May 2027. This is an estimate, not the confirmed kapat opening date.

    The later expected opening comes from the timing of Akshaya Tritiya. In 2027 it falls on 9 May, significantly later than in 2026. Kedarnath opens around the start of the Char Dham Yatra season, with the exact auspicious date set by religious tradition and the Hindu Panchang.

    Pilgrims should therefore plan for May rather than April. Browse a ready-to-go Kedarnath Yatra package from Delhi and confirm dates once the official announcement is out.

    How Is the Kedarnath Opening Date Decided?

    Unlike a fixed holiday, the Kedarnath Yatra opening date changes every year. It is calculated per the Hindu Panchang and customs of the shrine. The date and auspicious time are declared on Maha Shivratri at Omkareshwar Temple, Ukhimath, the winter seat of Baba Kedarnath. In 2027, Maha Shivratri falls on 6 March.

    Step 1

    Maha Shivratri Announcement

    The opening date and muhurat are declared at Omkareshwar Temple, Ukhimath.

    Step 2

    Panchmukhi Doli Departs

    The Panchmukhi Doli of Baba Kedarnath travels from Ukhimath towards Kedarnath Dham.

    Step 3

    Kapat Opening Ceremony

    After the Doli arrives and traditional rituals are completed, the temple doors are ceremonially reopened.

    Kedarnath Closing Date 2027

    The Kedarnath Temple closing date 2027 is also not announced yet. The temple traditionally closes around Bhai Dooj, shortly after Diwali, when winter makes the high-altitude region hard to reach. Expect closing around late October or early November 2027; the exact date and muhurat are announced near the end of the season.

    After the kapat closes, the ceremonial idol of Baba Kedarnath is taken to Omkareshwar Temple, Ukhimath, where winter worship continues until the shrine reopens.

    Kedarnath Yatra 2027 Registration

    Along with the opening date, watch for the Kedarnath Yatra registration 2027 announcement. Registration is an essential part of planning and should be completed through the official Uttarakhand Yatra registration portal once it opens. It generally needs basic traveller details and valid ID, and rules can change each season, so verify the final 2027 guidelines before travelling.

    Kedarnath Weather Around the Opening Date

    Kedarnath sits at high altitude in the Garhwal Himalayas, and May can still be extremely cold, especially early mornings and nights. Snow may remain around the surrounding mountains in the opening weeks, and weather can change fast. Carry warm layers, waterproof clothing, gloves and good trekking shoes.
