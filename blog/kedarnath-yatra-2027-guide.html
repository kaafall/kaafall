Located in the Rudraprayag district of Uttarakhand at an altitude of around 3,580 metres, Kedarnath Dham is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva and an important part of the Char Dham Yatra. The shrine stays inaccessible during the harsh winter and welcomes pilgrims during the annual yatra season. This Kedarnath travel guide 2027 covers the essentials you need before planning your pilgrimage.

    Kedarnath Temple Opening Date 2027

    The Kedarnath opening date 2027 has not yet been officially announced, so check with the official Uttarakhand tourism and temple authorities before finalising any bookings. Kedarnath generally opens during April or May and closes around October or November as winter approaches. For reference, the official Uttarakhand Char Dham portal listed 22 April as the Kedarnath Dham opening date for the 2026 season.

    Kedarnath Yatra Registration 2027

    Kedarnath Yatra registration is an important part of planning the pilgrimage. Uttarakhand's official Tourist Care portal states that registration is compulsory for the Char Dham Yatra and that pilgrims should keep their registration certificate ready for verification. The process generates a Yatra Registration Letter with a QR code.

    The usual online process involves creating an account, selecting the travel dates and dham, entering pilgrim details and downloading the registration letter. The portal currently lists Aadhaar card, voter ID, driving licence or passport as accepted ID proof. Registration itself is free.

    How to Reach Kedarnath – Complete Route

    There is no direct motorable road up to Kedarnath Temple. The road journey ends around Gaurikund, after which pilgrims continue on foot or use pony, palki or helicopter services. A common Kedarnath route from Delhi looks like this:

    Stop 1

    Delhi → Haridwar / Rishikesh

    Most pilgrims start the mountain journey from Haridwar or Rishikesh. Rishikesh is one of the main railway gateways, while Jolly Grant Airport near Dehradun is the nearest major airport.

    Stop 2

    Devprayag → Srinagar → Rudraprayag

    The road follows the Alaknanda valley through the Garhwal hills, with sangam points and river views along the way.

    Stop 3

    Guptkashi → Sonprayag → Gaurikund

    Guptkashi and Sonprayag are popular overnight stops before the trek, with hotels and guest houses for early starts.

    Road journey ends at Gaurikund
    Stop 4

    Gaurikund → Kedarnath

    The final section is covered on foot, or by pony, palki or helicopter where available.

    Destination: Kedarnath Dham

    According to the official Char Dham portal, Rishikesh to the Kedarnath roadhead is approximately 220 km by road, while Haridwar is around 240 km, followed by the trekking section.

    Gaurikund to Kedarnath Trek Distance & Route

    The Gaurikund to Kedarnath trek is the most physically demanding part of the yatra. The trek is approximately 24 km and usually takes 8–9 hours, though the exact distance can vary slightly with the active route and how it is measured. Uttarakhand Tourism describes it as a medium-to-steep high-altitude trek.

    Kedarnath Trek at a GlanceDetails
    Starting PointGaurikund
    Trek DistanceApprox. 24 km
    Typical Climb TimeUsually 8–9 hours
    DifficultyMedium to steep, high altitude
    AlternativesPony, palki and helicopter services

    The trail passes through several rest points before reaching Kedarnath. Fitness, weather, crowd levels and rest breaks can make the climb longer than 8–9 hours. Pilgrims who cannot comfortably complete the trek on foot can check officially authorised pony and palki services. Helicopter services are also operated from locations such as Phata, Sirsi and Guptkashi.

    Kedarnath Yatra Cost 2027 – How Much Budget Do You Need?

    The Kedarnath Yatra cost 2027 depends heavily on where you start, your transport choice, accommodation type and whether you walk or use helicopter, pony or palki services. For a regular budget trip with buses or shared transport and basic stays, keep approximately ₹6,000–₹12,000 per person for the core journey from Haridwar or Rishikesh. A trip from Delhi may need roughly ₹8,000–₹15,000+ per person, depending on travel style and number of nights. These are practical planning estimates, not official tariffs.

    Where to Stay During Kedarnath Yatra

    Kedarnath stay options range from hotels and guest houses around Guptkashi, Sitapur, Sonprayag and Gaurikund to dormitories, tents and government accommodation closer to the shrine.

    Guptkashi, Sitapur, Sonprayag & Gaurikund

    Hotels and guest houses at the base. Staying at Guptkashi or Sonprayag the night before lets you begin the trek early the next morning.

    Best Time to Visit Kedarnath & Essential Travel Tips

    For most travellers, the best time to visit Kedarnath is May and June, or after the monsoon in September and October. July and August can bring heavy rainfall, slippery trails and disruptions on mountain roads, while temperatures turn considerably colder towards the end of the season.

    Himalayan weather changes quickly, so pack smart:

    • Warm layers, a waterproof jacket, gloves and comfortable trekking shoes
    • Essential and prescribed medicines, water and light snacks
    • Valid ID and your downloaded registration letter
    • Walk at a comfortable pace, stay hydrated and avoid rushing the ascent

    Anyone with health concerns should take the altitude seriously. Kedarnath sits above 3,500 metres, and the combination of cold weather, steep climbing and reduced oxygen can make the trek demanding.
