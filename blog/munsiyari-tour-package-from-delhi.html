Looking for a Munsiyari tour package from Delhi that covers Himalayan views, sightseeing, comfortable stays, road travel and adventure? Munsiyari is a mountain destination in Pithoragarh district, Uttarakhand, known for spectacular views of the Panchachuli peaks – an excellent choice beyond the usual crowded hill stations.

A well-planned Munsiyari tour package can combine local sightseeing with the Khaliya Top trek, camping, waterfalls, temples and traditional Himalayan villages. Whether it's a family holiday, couple trip or a Munsiyari group tour from Delhi, choosing the right itinerary matters because the journey is long and attractions are spread out.

    Why Choose Munsiyari for Your Next Uttarakhand Trip?

    Munsiyari offers mountain scenery, Kumaoni culture, trekking and peaceful surroundings. It lies in the Gori Ganga valley and is historically a gateway towards the Johar Valley and high-altitude trekking routes. A Munsiyari Uttarakhand tour package can include nature, adventure and local experiences in one trip.

    • Sunrise and sunset over the Panchachuli Peaks
    • Trekking to Khaliya Top (also searched as Khalia Top)
    • Birthi Falls and Kalamuni
    • Darkot and nearby Himalayan villages
    • Nanda Devi Temple and local attractions
    • Trekking and camping around Munsiyari
    • Snow-covered landscapes in suitable winter conditions

    For first-timers, a Munsiyari complete tour package with hotel, transport and sightseeing makes planning much easier.

    Top Attractions Covered in Our Munsiyari Trip

    Our Munsiyari tour package from Delhi combines spiritual destinations, scenic Himalayan drives, waterfalls, mountain views, trekking and camping. The journey covers some of the most popular attractions across the Kumaon region.

    1

    Kainchi Dham

    Begin your Himalayan journey with a visit to Kainchi Dham, the famous Neem Karoli Baba Ashram near Nainital. Surrounded by forested hills, the peaceful temple complex is one of the most visited spiritual destinations in Uttarakhand.

    2

    Jageshwar Dham

    Explore Jageshwar Dham, an ancient Shiva temple complex surrounded by dense deodar forests near Almora. The historic stone temples, peaceful environment and mountain setting make it an important spiritual stop on the way to Munsiyari.

    3

    Munsiyari

    Discover Munsiyari, a beautiful hill destination in the Pithoragarh district of Uttarakhand. Located in the Kumaon Himalayas, the town is famous for its views of the Panchachuli Peaks, mountain landscapes, trekking trails and peaceful surroundings.

    4

    Birthi Falls

    Stop at Birthi Falls while travelling towards Munsiyari. The waterfall descends through a lush mountain landscape and provides a refreshing break during the road journey, with excellent opportunities for nature photography.

    5

    Khaliya Top Trek & Camping

    Experience one of the highlights of the trip with the Khaliya Top Trek. Trek through forest trails and high-altitude meadows while enjoying panoramic Himalayan scenery. Our package also includes a camping experience near Khaliya Top, subject to local weather and trail conditions.

    6

    Khaliya Top Zero Point

    Continue towards Zero Point for expansive views of the surrounding Himalayan ranges. The visit depends on weather, snow, trail conditions and guidance from the local trekking team.

    7

    Panchachuli Peaks

    Witness the spectacular Panchachuli Peaks, one of the defining sights of Munsiyari. On clear days, these snow-covered Himalayan peaks create impressive sunrise, sunset and photography views from different locations around Munsiyari.

    8

    Nanda Devi Temple

    Visit the Nanda Devi Temple in Munsiyari, a revered local temple surrounded by beautiful Himalayan scenery. The peaceful location also provides impressive views of the surrounding mountains.

    5-Day Munsiyari Tour Itinerary from Delhi

    Day 1

    Delhi / Kathgodam → Kainchi Dham → Jageshwar

    Start your Munsiyari tour from Delhi and travel towards the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand. Guests joining from Kathgodam can meet the group at the designated pickup location.

    On the way, visit Kainchi Dham, the renowned Neem Karoli Baba Ashram surrounded by the beautiful hills of Kumaon.

    Continue towards Jageshwar Dham and explore the ancient Shiva temple complex nestled among tall deodar forests.

    After sightseeing, check in to your accommodation and relax.

    Night Stay: Jageshwar
    Day 2

    Jageshwar → Birthi Falls → Munsiyari

    After breakfast, check out and begin the scenic journey towards Munsiyari.

    Drive through the mountain roads of Kumaon and stop at Birthi Falls on the way. Spend some time enjoying the waterfall and surrounding landscape before continuing towards Munsiyari.

    On arrival, check in to your hotel. In clear weather, enjoy beautiful views of the Panchachuli Peaks and spend the evening exploring the peaceful surroundings.

    Night Stay: Munsiyari
    Day 3

    Munsiyari → Khaliya Top Trek & Camping

    After breakfast, get ready for the Khaliya Top Trek, one of the major adventure experiences included in the Munsiyari package.

    Accompanied by a local trekking guide, follow the mountain trail through forests, open meadows and high-altitude landscapes.

    Reach the designated camping area and settle into your camp. Spend the evening surrounded by the Himalayan landscape and enjoy sunset views if weather conditions are favourable.

    Night Stay: Khaliya Top Camp
    Day 4

    Khaliya Top → Zero Point → Munsiyari → Pithoragarh

    Wake up early and enjoy the mountain sunrise, subject to weather conditions.

    After breakfast, proceed towards Khaliya Top Zero Point when weather, snow and trail conditions permit. Spend some time enjoying panoramic Himalayan views before beginning the descent.

    Trek back towards Munsiyari and later continue your road journey to Pithoragarh.

    Check in and rest after the day's journey.

    Night Stay: Pithoragarh
    Day 5

    Pithoragarh → Kathgodam / Delhi

    After breakfast, check out and begin the return journey through the beautiful Kumaon hills.

    Travel towards Kathgodam and Delhi, enjoying mountain roads, valleys and changing landscapes along the way.

    Your 5-day Munsiyari tour package from Delhi concludes with memories of Kainchi Dham, Jageshwar Dham, Birthi Falls, Munsiyari, Panchachuli Peaks and the Khaliya Top trekking and camping experience.

    Khaliya Top Trek and Camping in Munsiyari

    Khaliya Top Munsiyari is one of the region's major trekking attractions. The trek starts from the Balati Farm/Balanti area and climbs through Himalayan terrain to open high-altitude meadows with expansive views.

    A Munsiyari package with Khaliya Top trek suits friends, adventure groups and active travellers. Depending on operator, season, permissions and weather, it may include Khaliya Top camping. Trek distance, difficulty, temperature and snow vary by season – snow makes the trail much more demanding.

    • Transfer to the trek starting area
    • Guided trek and trekking support where offered
    • Meals as per package
    • Camping where operationally and legally permitted
    • Return trek and transfer to Munsiyari

    Before choosing a Khaliya Top trek package from Delhi, confirm campsite arrangement, guide, meals, equipment, weather policy and whether camping is available on your dates. For trek rules, also check the Uttarakhand Tourism website.
