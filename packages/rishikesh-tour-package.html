Rishikesh Tour Guide: Price, Best Time & How to Reach

Rishikesh is a holy town on the banks of the Ganga in Dehradun district, Uttarakhand, known as the "Yoga Capital of the World" and the adventure capital of India. It is one of the most popular short getaways from Delhi.

Rishikesh Tour Package Price From Delhi

Kafall Travels offers Rishikesh tour packages from Delhi for 2 Nights 3 Days starting at ₹5,200 per person (Basic), ₹6,500 (Standard) and ₹7,800 (Premium). Packages include a private cab from Delhi, stay, meals as per plan, rafting, Ganga aarti and 24/7 assistance. 5% GST is extra. Need only a car? Check our Delhi to Rishikesh taxi service.

Best Time to Visit Rishikesh

October to March has pleasant weather, ideal for sightseeing, yoga and camping. March to June is warmer but is the peak rafting season. July to mid-September is monsoon, when rafting is usually closed and river conditions are unsafe.

How to Reach Rishikesh From Delhi

By road: Delhi → Meerut → Roorkee → Haridwar → Rishikesh, about 240 km in 5–6 hours. By train: take a train to Haridwar (about 25 km away) or Yog Nagri Rishikesh, then a cab. By air: Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun is the nearest airport, about 35 km from Rishikesh. Kafall's private cab picks you up from Delhi and drops you back.

Top Things to Do in Rishikesh

White-water rafting on the Ganga, evening Ganga aarti at Triveni Ghat or Parmarth Niketan, Laxman Jhula and Ram Jhula walks, sunrise yoga and meditation, Neer Garh waterfall trek, Neelkanth Mahadev temple, riverside camping and bonfire nights.
